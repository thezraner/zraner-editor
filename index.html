Zraner Editor

Herramienta personal de escritorio con la que el creador del canal de YouTube @Zraner termina de publicar sus propios vídeos de gameplay: sube sus Shorts ya editados a su canal como privados (él los revisa y los publica después) y publica en sus propios vídeos un comentario escrito por él. Personal desktop tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel @Zraner to finish publishing his own gameplay videos: it uploads his already-edited Shorts to his channel as private (he reviews and publishes them later) and posts a comment written by him on his own videos. Everything happens only after he confirms it. It is not a service for other people and it does not collect anyone's data.
